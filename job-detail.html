Content Writer

Lagos, Nigeria Full-time 14 August 2026

About the role

Tell the stories of Nigerian businesses, people and ideas across our editorial channels. As a Content Writer at Connect Nigeria, you'll create compelling written content that informs, engages and inspires our audience.

You'll work closely with the product, marketing and design teams to produce content that drives engagement and supports business goals. From blog posts and social media copy to product descriptions and email campaigns, you'll have the opportunity to shape how Connect Nigeria communicates with the world.

Key responsibilities

  • Write clear, engaging and SEO-optimised content for blog posts, landing pages and social media
  • Research Nigerian businesses, industries and trends to produce informed editorial pieces
  • Collaborate with designers and marketers to create compelling campaigns
  • Edit and proofread content to maintain brand voice and quality standards
  • Track content performance and suggest improvements based on data
  • Contribute to content strategy and editorial calendar planning

Requirements

  • 2+ years of professional content writing experience
  • Excellent written English with strong grammar and storytelling skills
  • Experience with SEO best practices and content management systems
  • Ability to manage multiple projects and meet deadlines
  • Familiarity with Nigerian business landscape is a plus

Benefits

  • Competitive salary and performance bonuses
  • Remote-friendly work environment
  • Health insurance and wellness benefits
  • Professional development opportunities
  • Flexible working hours
